Work instruction WI-27: replace the gripper fingers on robot cell

Use when RB551 keeps coming back or a part drops at pick-up.

  1. Stop the cell and open the light curtain muting key switch.
  2. Remove the two worn gripper finger with the 4 mm key.
  3. Fit new GRP FNG 12 and torque to 6 Nm.
  4. Run ten pick cycles and check that RB551 does not return.
